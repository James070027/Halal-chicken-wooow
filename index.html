2020 - Katabi: We started small in Katabi, Entebbe, with 20 broilers, selling to neighbours and a WhatsApp group. We learned halal slaughter from an elder.

Local Eggs (Kienyeji)

A local hen lays about 1 egg every 2 days, compared with daily for exotic layers, so the price is higher. Natural feed, deep yellow yolk.


Egg Tips

Exotic eggs: Good for the chapati and cake business. For chapati rolex, beat 2 eggs with salt, chopped onions and tomatoes and fry for 2 minutes. Store the tray in a cool place and use within 2 weeks.

Local eggs: Popular for children and pregnant mothers. Boil for 10 minutes, or fry with tomatoes and onions for 3 minutes.

Freshness check: Put an egg in water. If it sinks flat it is very fresh; if it sinks and stands up it is about a week old; if it floats, do not use it.

How to Rear Chicks

Housing: Keep chicks in a dry house with no wind, about 3x3 ft for 50 chicks, with wood shavings about 2 inches thick, cleaned daily. Exotic day-old chicks need a 100W bulb at about 32°C in week one, reduced by about 2°C each week. Hardened local chicks need no heat.

Feeds: Exotic chicks: starter feed for weeks 0-2, grower for weeks 2-5, finisher after week 5. Local chicks: starter until 4 weeks, grower until 8 weeks, then free-range with maize, greens and mukene. Always give clean water; vitamins in the first 5 days and glucose on day one.

Vaccines: Follow the schedule in the guide book that comes with your chicks (Marek, Newcastle, Gumboro). Ask us on WhatsApp for advice at any time.

Which to choose: For fast meat (about 5 weeks) choose exotic broiler chicks. For daily eggs choose exotic layer chicks. For a strong, low-cost free-range flock with no brooder, choose local one-month chicks.
